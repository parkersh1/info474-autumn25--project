Every day, thousands of drivers take to the road without thinking about how weather, road design, or small decisions behind the wheel can change the outcome of a drive. In this interactive article, we explore patterns and relationships in real traffic incident data from Washington State to uncover how environmental and behavioral factors, such as rain and intersection type, can influence both the likelihood and severity of accidents. By visualizing these trends, we aim to give drivers a clearer understanding of the risk factors involved and help promote safer, more mindful driving habits.

Accident Frequency Among Traffic Intersection Types
This chart shows how often accidents in Washington State involved specific intersection or traffic features (e.g., stop signs, roundabouts, traffic signals). Perhaps unsurprisingly, there were significantly less accidents when safer traffic features were involved, such as speed bumps, roundabouts, and specific traffic calming signs. Based off this chart, one can compare which intersection types and traffic controls are commonly present in collisions and to inform safer driving choices—for example, by paying extra attention where the data shows higher frequencies.
